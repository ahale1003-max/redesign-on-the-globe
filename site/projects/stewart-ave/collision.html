Who We Are

Central Wisconsin's Preferred Auto Body Shop

Stewart Ave Collision & Repair is central Wisconsin's preferred auto body shop and collision repair center, conveniently located on Stewart Avenue in Wausau. You can trust our staff to get your vehicle back on the road fast by offering bumper-to-bumper services — including all auto body and mechanical repairs needed as a result of your accident, all under one roof.

From a minor scratch or dent to major auto body and collision repair, our experienced, friendly, and professional staff will help you through the entire collision repair process — from preparing an estimate, to the insurance claim and appraisal process, to completing your repair.

We work with ALL INSURANCE COMPANIES and will work within your budget to get your vehicle back on the road as soon as possible.

FREE LOANER CARS are available and all auto body and mechanical repairs performed by Stewart Ave Collision & Repair are covered by a LIFETIME WARRANTY for as long as you own the vehicle.
