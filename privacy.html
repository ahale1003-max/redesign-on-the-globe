Privacy policy

The short version.

We collect what you type into our contact form, and we use it to get back to you. No tracking, no ad pixels, no selling your details. The longer version is below.

Effective date: [add date before launch]

01Who we are

On The Globe Marketing.

We're a small marketing studio based in the United States. This policy covers this website only. When it says “we” or “us,” it means On The Globe Marketing.

02What we collect

Only what you give us.

  • 01Your first and last name
  • 02Your email address or your phone number — whichever you choose to share
  • 03Anything you write in the optional message box

That's the whole list, and it only happens when you press send on the contact form. Just browsing the site doesn't create an account, a profile or a record with us.

03Why we collect it

To reply to you.

We use your details to answer your message and, if we end up working together, to stay in touch about that work. We don't add you to a mailing list and we don't send marketing emails you didn't ask for.

We never sell, rent or trade your information.

04Who else is involved

Three services help run this site.

  • 01FormSubmit delivers the contact form to our inbox. What you type passes through their servers on the way to us.
  • 02Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser fetches them from Google when a page loads.
  • 03jsDelivr supplies the map code and map outlines on the home and Work pages.

Like any website request, loading files from these services shares your IP address and basic browser details with them. Each has its own privacy policy. Messages you send us land in our Gmail inbox, which is provided by Google.

05Cookies & tracking

None.

This site doesn't set cookies, doesn't run analytics and doesn't use advertising or social media tracking pixels. If that ever changes, we'll update this page first.

06Client sites shown here

The previews are demonstrations.

Our Work pages let you click around copies of websites we've built. Those copies are there to show the work. Forms inside a preview are not connected to that business, so please don't use them to book or contact our clients — visit their own website instead.

07How long we keep it

As long as it's useful, then it's gone.

We keep your message while we're in conversation and for as long as we're working together. If nothing comes of it, we delete it during our regular inbox clean-ups. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

08Your choices

Ask, and we'll do it.

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email ontheglobemarketing@gmail.com and we'll take care of it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy law; we'll honor those too.

This website is meant for business owners and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

09Changes

We'll keep this current.

If what we collect or how we use it changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions about any of it? Email us — a real person answers.
